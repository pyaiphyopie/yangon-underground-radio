## Logo Files

## Colors
- Background / Black: `#070707`
- White: `#FFFFFF` / `#F5F7FA`
- Electric Cyan: `#00AEEF`
- Neon Violet: `#8B5CF6`

## Usage
- Prefer dark backgrounds.
- Maintain clear space around the mark.
- Do not stretch or recolor the cyan/violet elements without approval.
- For social and stream overlays, export PNG from the SVG at 2x or 3x.
