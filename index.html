About

Yangon Underground Radio is an independent internet radio station focused on underground hip-hop, rock, alternative, metal, electronic music, and DJ mixes from Yangon and the region. Burmese + English. Curation and scene access over library size.

Public launch is conditional on regulatory verification and music rights clearance. Private testing first.

Artist Submissions

Independent artists and DJs: submit tracks with documented permission for live internet radio, archive, and promotional clips. Contact via station channels once live.
